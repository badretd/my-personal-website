1. Purpose

This website is the personal website of Badretdinov I. R.

It presents the author as:

a programmer;
a musician;
a beginning director;
a participant in olympiads;
the creator of personal technical and creative projects.

The website should not behave like a formal résumé.

Its purpose is to introduce the author, establish a recognizable identity, and provide access to projects, music, blog posts, personal information, and contacts.
